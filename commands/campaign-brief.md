---
description: Produce a scoping brief for a marketing campaign before any copy gets written
---

Produce a campaign brief — the marketing equivalent of a technical scoping doc, written before any actual copy exists. Ground it in the real product and real audience (delegate to `growth-strategist` if the target segment or funnel constraint isn't already clear), not generic campaign-template filler.

1. **Objective** — the one thing this campaign needs to move (signups, activation, a specific feature's adoption), stated as a number or clearly observable outcome, not "raise awareness."
2. **Audience** — who specifically this is for, and what they already believe/know/use today that the campaign has to work with or against.
3. **The one claim** — the single most true, most differentiated thing this campaign leads with. If it's not settled, say so and stop here rather than scoping assets against an unclear claim.
4. **Assets required**, each mapped to an owner and a rough word count or format (landing section, 3 emails, 2 social posts) — not a vague "some copy for X."
5. **Timeline and dependencies** — what has to exist before what (assets before the send date, landing page live before any post links to it).
6. **Success metric** — the specific number and the specific place it'll be measured, stated before the campaign runs.

Output a short, ordered brief — not finished copy. Once the brief is approved, hand asset-writing to `content-marketer` and voice/claim-checking to `brand-voice`.
