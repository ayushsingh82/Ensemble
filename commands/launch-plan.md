---
description: Produce a concrete launch plan for a feature or product once it's ready to ship
---

Produce a launch plan for what's being shipped. Ground it in what the product actually does right now — read the code/README if the claims aren't already clear — rather than generic launch-checklist filler.

1. **The one claim.** What's the single most true, most differentiated thing to lead with? (Delegate to `growth-strategist` if this isn't already clear.)
2. **Channels, in priority order.** Where does the actual target user already spend attention — not every channel that exists. Justify each one in one sentence.
3. **Assets needed**, mapped to owner: landing copy, launch post, email sequence — call `content-marketer` for the actual copy once the claim and channels are set.
4. **Sequencing** — what goes out first, what depends on what (e.g. landing page live before the launch post links to it).
5. **What "worked" looks like** — the specific metric and rough threshold that would tell you this launch succeeded, stated before launch, not after.

Output a short, ordered plan — not a document. If positioning isn't settled yet, say so and stop here rather than writing copy against an unclear claim.
