---
description: Structure a multi-email sequence (onboarding, launch, nurture) with one claim per email
---

Structure an email sequence — the send order, purpose, and single claim of each email — before any full email gets drafted.

1. **State the sequence's overall goal** (activate a new signup, launch a feature, win back a lapsed user) and how many emails it actually needs — don't default to a fixed number; a sequence that needs 3 emails and gets padded to 6 wastes the reader's attention on the later ones.
2. **For each email, in order**: its single job (what should the reader do or believe after reading it), its one claim, and how it connects to the email before it — a sequence where email 3 doesn't reference or build on email 2 isn't really a sequence.
3. **One claim per email**, matching `content-marketer`'s discipline — an email trying to make three points is usually an email that should be two emails or a shorter one.
4. **State the trigger and timing for each send** (immediately on signup, 24h later, on a specific in-app event) — a sequence with no stated trigger logic can't actually be implemented.
5. **State what "worked" means for the sequence** — the specific metric (activation rate, click-through, reply rate) and roughly what the sends are trying to move, before any email gets sent.

Output the structure — subject-line direction, one-line claim, and send trigger for each email — not full drafted copy. Hand off to `content-marketer` for the actual email text once the structure is set, and `brand-voice` to check voice/claims before sending.
